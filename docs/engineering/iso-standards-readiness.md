`iso-standards-readiness` prepares and structurally reviews readiness evidence for ISO management-system and laboratory-competence standards - ISO 13485 medical device QMS, ISO 14971 device risk management, ISO/IEC 17025 testing and calibration laboratories, and ISO 15189 medical laboratories

The result has to name its inputs and the assumption that would change it. Ordinary software work stays with the house skills.
