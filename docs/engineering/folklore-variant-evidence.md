## What it does

`folklore-variant-evidence` retrieves ClinGen gene-disease validity assertions for a public gene or disease, and reviews source-linked public evidence and literature for one supported GRCh38 germline nuclear SNV or simple indel through Folklore Clinical Variant Interpretation MCP

The result has to name its inputs and the assumption that would change it. Ordinary software work stays with the house skills.
