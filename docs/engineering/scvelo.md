## What it does

`scvelo` performs RNA velocity analysis with scVelo from spliced and unspliced single-cell RNA counts. Fits deterministic or dynamical models, examines gene phase portraits, builds velocity graphs, estimates relative latent time, and ranks velocity-associated genes

The result has to name its inputs and the assumption that would change it. Ordinary software work stays with the house skills.
