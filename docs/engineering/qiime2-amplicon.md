## What it does

`qiime2-amplicon` processes paired-end 16S amplicon reads into QIIME 2 ASVs and taxonomy with retained artifact provenance. Checks paired FASTQ manifests, primer orientation diagnostics, predicted post-trimming overlap, sample IDs, runtime versions and read retention, and guides selection of compatible taxonomic classifiers.
