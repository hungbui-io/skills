## What it does

`scientific-writing` drafts, revises, and audits scientific manuscripts or reports with explicit evidence provenance, reporting-guideline coverage, authorship accountability, confidentiality controls, and local consistency checks

House boundary: documents that agents read are writing-for-agents. This skill drafts a scientific manuscript or report.

## When to reach for it

Type `/scientific-writing`, or the [agent](https://www.aihero.dev/ai-coding-dictionary/agent) reaches for it when a task fits.

Use for manuscript sections, references, declarations, tables, figures, or submission preparation when scientific accuracy and traceability matter.
