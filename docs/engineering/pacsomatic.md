## What it does

`pacsomatic` prepares and launches nf-core/pacsomatic matched tumor-normal PacBio HiFi genomics workflows from unaligned BAM inputs. Supports samplesheet generation, pinned Nextflow launch artifacts, local checks, LSF/Slurm/PBS Pro/SGE launcher submission, and startup troubleshooting

The result has to name its inputs and the assumption that would change it. Ordinary software work stays with the house skills.
