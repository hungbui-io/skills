## What it does

`diffdock` predicts protein-small-molecule binding poses with DiffDock and DiffDock-L from PDB or sequence plus SMILES/SDF/MOL2. Covers batch docking, pose triage, confidence interpretation, and validation

The result has to name its inputs and the assumption that would change it. Ordinary software work stays with the house skills.
